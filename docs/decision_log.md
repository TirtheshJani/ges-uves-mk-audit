# Decisions log: stellar-mk-audit

Binding decisions for the LightGBM MK audit paper. Every decision that affects the paper's claims, methodology, or reproducibility is logged here with date, reasoning, and what would reverse it.

This file is the reproducibility contract. Phase 6 methods writing pulls from here. The Physicist subagent (see `auditplan` §4.5) verifies entries against canonical references.

Citation conventions: author + year for papers; bibcode for unambiguous reference; VizieR code for catalogs; NIST ASD for atomic data. Unverified citations are tagged `[CITE NEEDED]` and must be resolved before Phase 6.

No em dashes anywhere in this file.

---

## Entry format

```
## N. Short title

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision:** what was chosen
- **Reasoning:** why, with citations
- **Reversal:** condition that would require revisiting this
- **Affects:** files, phases, or artifacts touched
```

When a decision is reversed, append a new entry rather than editing the original. Example: `## 14. REVERSED decision 8 on 2026-05-02 after Physicist review`.

## Strategic decisions (Phase 0 scope)

These four decisions set the paper's scope. They were baked into the initial scaffolded code before `decisions.md` existed; Phase -1 logs them retroactively. Phase 0 probes are the first opportunity to reverse any of them.

## 1. Wavelength window locked to 4800-6800 A (Option A)

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** feature window is 4800-6800 A, corresponding to UVES U580 nominal coverage.
- **Reasoning:** UVES U580 native coverage is approximately 4760-6840 A (Dekker et al. 2000; Sacco et al. 2014, A&A 565, A113). Staying within U580 avoids combining U520 and U580 setups, which would require separate normalization and reduce the cross-matched sample. The window covers all five diagnostic features claimed in the paper: Hbeta (4861.33 A), Mg b triplet (5167.32, 5172.68, 5183.60 A), Na D doublet (5889.95, 5895.92 A), Ca I (6162.17, 6439.08 A), and Halpha (6562.80 A). All wavelengths in air, per NIST ASD (Kramida et al. 2023).
- **Reversal:** Phase 0 coverage probe reports `n_covered < 2000` spectra meeting the 90 percent coverage threshold for this window.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/features.py` (`DEFAULT_WAVE_MIN`, `DEFAULT_WAVE_MAX`); `src/interpret/lines.py` (`MK_LINES`, `LINE_SETS`); `project_plan.md` Phase 0 Decision 1.

## 2. Label scope locked to A/F/G/K

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** classifier is trained only on A, F, G, K classes. O, B, M excluded at label construction time.
- **Reasoning:** the 4800-6800 A window excludes TiO bands at 7050-7100 A required for M-class diagnosis, and He II at 4686 A required for O/B classification (Gray & Corbally 2009, Stellar Spectral Classification). GES UVES is FGK-targeted by design (Gilmore et al. 2022, A&A 666, A120), so O/B/M sample counts would be too low to train on even if the window permitted.
- **Reversal:** Phase 0 class counts show A below 50 spectra (then drop A from the class set rather than widen the window); or decision 1 is reversed.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/labels.py` (`MK_BIN_EDGES`, `ALLOWED_MK_CLASSES`); `src/interpret/lines.py` (`diag_for` attributions).

## 3. External benchmark locked to Pickles 1998 UVKLIB

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** benchmark the classifier against chi-squared template matching using the Pickles UVKLIB stellar flux library (Pickles 1998, PASP 110, 863; VizieR J/PASP/110/863).
- **Reasoning:** independent physics, not the same pipeline that generated training labels. This is the most defensible of the three benchmark options in `project_plan.md` Phase 0 Decision 2 and survives the "tested on the labels that trained it" critique that Li, Lin and Qiu (2019) did not address. Options A (GES DR5 `sp_type` column, if present) and C (SIMBAD `sp_type`) were rejected because both inherit pipeline or literature heterogeneity.
- **Reversal:** Pickles UVKLIB FITS files are unavailable locally and remote download mirrors are blocked.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/benchmark.py`; `scripts/run_benchmark.py`; Phase 5 execution.

## 4. GES parameters catalog locked to VizieR J/A+A/666/A121

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** pull Teff, logg, [Fe/H] for MK labeling from the Gaia-ESO DR5 recommended-parameters catalog (Hourihane et al. 2023, A&A 666, A121; bibcode 2023A&A...666A.121H; VizieR J/A+A/666/A121).
- **Reasoning:** Hourihane et al. 2023 is the authoritative GES DR5 recommended-parameters table, providing Teff with the precision needed for MK binning via Pecaut & Mamajek (2013) edges. The alternative J/A+A/692/A228 mentioned in the original plan text was considered for a direct spectral-type column but is not required given Teff-based binning.
- **Reversal:** VizieR does not resolve J/A+A/666/A121, or the resolved table's columns do not map to the aliases in `src/interpret/labels.py._VIZIER_ALIASES` (RA, Dec, Teff, logg, [Fe/H]).
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/labels.py` (`VIZIER_CATALOG`, `VIZIER_BIBCODE`, `fetch_ges_params_catalog`); `data/ges/catalogs/ges_dr5_params.parquet` cache.

## Hyperparameter and threshold anchors

Lower-stakes decisions committed in code. Logged here for reproducibility and Physicist verification. These should not change unless a Physicist review flags a mismatch with canonical values.

## 5. MK Teff bin edges

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** A [7300, 10000) K; F [6000, 7300) K; G [5300, 6000) K; K [3900, 5300) K.
- **Reasoning:** Pecaut & Mamajek (2013) ApJS 208, 9, Table 5, rounded to the nearest 100 K. Standard dwarf-sequence bin edges for MK classification. Giant-branch discrepancies from Gray & Corbally (2009) are below 100 K at these boundaries and ignored here since the classifier is dwarf-dominated (UVES-FGK sample).
- **Reversal:** Physicist review identifies a post-2013 calibration update that shifts any boundary by more than 100 K.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/labels.py` (`MK_BIN_EDGES`).

## 6. Cross-match radii

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** primary match radius 0.5 arcsec; ambiguity (second-nearest) cutoff 2.0 arcsec.
- **Reasoning:** 0.5 arcsec is a conservative tolerance given Gaia DR3 astrometric precision and the GES pointing convention. The 2.0 arcsec ambiguity cutoff prevents wrong-star assignment in crowded open-cluster fields where GES is concentrated.
- **Reversal:** Phase 1 diagnostics show more than 5 percent ambiguous matches at 2.0 arcsec, indicating a density regime that requires tighter cuts.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/labels.py` (`build_labels` defaults).

## 7. Signal-to-noise floor

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** require median SNR >= 20 per pixel before features are built.
- **Reasoning:** SNR 20 is adequate for stellar classification (MK typing); SNR 40 would be required for [Fe/H] precision at +/- 0.1 dex, which is not our task. The 20-per-pixel floor matches the GES DR5.1 QC convention as cited in `src/interpret/features.py` docstring attributing it to Gilmore et al. 2022. `[CITE NEEDED: verify Gilmore+ 2022 specifically states SNR >= 20 as the DR5.1 QC floor for MK classification]`.
- **Reversal:** Phase 1 surviving-spectrum count drops below 1500 (approaching the Phase 0 kill threshold). Consider lowering to 15 only after Physicist review.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/features.py` (`DEFAULT_MIN_SNR`).

## 8. Rebin factor

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** rebin native UVES pixels by 5, producing ~2.75 A bins and ~1000 features per spectrum.
- **Reasoning:** native UVES pixel size is approximately 0.55 A. Rebinning by 5 produces bins narrower than the Mg b triplet component separations (5-11 A between b1, b2, b3) and narrower than typical line FWHMs at R ~ 47000. The resulting feature count is tractable for SHAP TreeExplainer within the Phase 3 time budget.
- **Reversal:** Physicist review in Phase 1 finds that Na D1 (5895.92 A) and Na D2 (5889.95 A), separated by 5.97 A, fall into the same rebinned bin with centers that prevent distinguishing them, OR Mg b1 and Mg b2 (separation 5.36 A) collapse into one bin.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/features.py` (`DEFAULT_REBIN`).

## 9. Coverage threshold

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** require >= 90 percent of native pixels in the feature window to be valid (finite, non-zero) for a spectrum to survive.
- **Reasoning:** the UVES U580 inter-chip gap at approximately 5769-5834 A spans ~3.25 percent of the 4800-6800 A window (Sacco et al. 2014). A 90 percent threshold tolerates this gap plus small detector cosmetics without admitting spectra with large missing regions.
- **Reversal:** none expected; this is an instrument-driven threshold.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/features.py` (`DEFAULT_COVERAGE_THRESHOLD`).

## 10. Post-rebin NaN cap

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** after rebinning, drop spectra with more than 10 percent NaN bins.
- **Reasoning:** the 90 percent coverage threshold applies before rebinning; this cap is a post-rebin cross-check. Remaining NaN bins after the cap are filled via train-set median imputation (decision 11).
- **Reversal:** per-class counts drop below Phase 0 minima after applying this cap.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/features.py` (`DEFAULT_MAX_NAN_FRAC`).

## 11. Imputation strategy

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** replace NaN bins with train-set per-bin median. Imputer is fit on training rows only and applied to validation and test.
- **Reasoning:** avoids label leakage. Follows The Cannon (Ness et al. 2015, ApJ 808, 16) and StarNet (Fabbro et al. 2018, MNRAS 475, 2978) conventions for spectrum-level imputation. Train-only medians preserve test-set independence.
- **Reversal:** Reviewer or Physicist identifies a leakage path; or per-bin medians are dominated by gap bins such that the imputed value is systematically biased.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/features.py` (`fit_median_imputer`, `apply_median_imputer`).

## 12. Stratified subsample cap

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** subsample the full UVES-matched set to at most 5000 spectra, stratified by MK class.
- **Reasoning:** keeps training, SHAP computation, and Phase 4 bootstrap budgets tractable on a laptop-class machine. Bootstrap CIs in Phase 4 are valid at this sample size. The paper's honest limitations section will note that Candebat et al. 2024 used 50K spectra.
- **Reversal:** Phase 4 bootstrap CIs exceed the target width of 0.05 on delta_acc, preventing the 2-of-3 headline gate from passing. Raise to 10000.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/features.py` (`build_features` `max_spectra` default); `scripts/build_features.py`.

## 13. LightGBM hyperparameters

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** `max_depth=8`, `num_leaves=63`, `learning_rate=0.05`, `n_estimators=500` with `early_stopping_rounds=50`, `min_child_samples=20`, `subsample=0.9`, `subsample_freq=1`, `colsample_bytree=0.9`, `class_weight='balanced'`, `random_state=42`.
- **Reasoning:** shallow trees (max_depth 8) keep TreeSHAP tractable in Phase 3. `class_weight='balanced'` compensates for FGK skew. Early stopping on validation loss prevents overfitting within the 500-tree budget. Fixed seed for reproducibility. These are standard LightGBM defaults for modest-sized tabular classification, not tuned.
- **Reversal:** Phase 2 kill criterion (macro-F1 < 0.5); OR SHAP computation exceeds 2 hours per class in Phase 3 (reduce `max_depth` or `num_leaves`).
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/classifier.py` (`DEFAULT_HPARAMS`).

## 14. Ablation bootstrap and null-distribution sizes

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** 500 bootstrap resamples of the test set for delta-accuracy CI; 100 random-window controls per line set, width-matched to each `LINE_SETS` entry and drawn outside all `MK_LINES` windows.
- **Reasoning:** 500 bootstrap resamples give CI half-widths stable to within ~1 percent at 95 percent confidence. 100 random controls give p-value resolution of 0.01, which is the Phase 4 gate threshold. Forbidden mask covers all `MK_LINES` windows to prevent inadvertent overlap with diagnostic features.
- **Reversal:** fewer than 80 of 100 random-window draws succeed due to tight forbidden-mask coverage; lower to 50 controls with explicit documentation, or relax forbidden mask to `MK_LINES +/- 10 A` instead of the full `LINE_SETS`.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/occlusion.py` (`masked_line_ablation` defaults); `scripts/ablation.py` CLI.

## 15. Wavelength convention

- **Date:** 2026-04-18
- **Decision:** all wavelengths in code, comments, and outputs are in AIR, matching the GES UVES pipeline convention (Sacco et al. 2014).
- **Reasoning:** mixing air and vacuum is the single most common source of 1-3 A offsets in spectral analysis code. Locking AIR at the decisions-log level gives the Physicist agent a clear standard to enforce at every phase review.
- **Reversal:** none. Any vacuum-wavelength quantity encountered in external data must be converted to air before use.
- **Affects:** everything downstream of `src/fetch/fetch_ges.py`. Particularly `src/interpret/lines.py` and `src/interpret/line_match.py`.

## 18. E_TEFF <= 200 K quality cut at Phase 1 label build

- **Date:** 2026-04-29
- **Decision:** at Phase 1 label-build time (NOT in this Phase 0a refactor), reject catalog rows whose recommended Teff uncertainty exceeds 200 K. Persist `e_teff_k` in the cache parquet so this cut can be applied in `build_labels` without re-fetching.
- **Reasoning:** We adopt E_TEFF <= 200 K as a sensible quality cut. Pecaut & Mamajek 2013 (Table 5) places adjacent MK Teff boundaries at spacings of 700 K (G/K) up to 2700 K (A/F); a 200 K precision cut keeps Teff uncertainty well below the narrowest boundary. The threshold is informed by, not prescribed by, the GES DR5.1 E_TEFF distribution (Hourihane et al. 2023, A&A 676, A129).
- **Reversal:** surviving sample after the cut drops below 1,500 rows (auditplan §5.3 Phase 1 kill criterion). At that point, relax to 300 K with explicit re-evaluation against the bin-edge spacing.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/labels.py` (`build_labels`; cut to be added in Phase 1, NOT in Phase 0a). The Phase 0a cache schema persists `e_teff_k` so no re-fetch is required.

## 19. REC_SETUP filter LIKE '%580%' at TAP query time

- **Date:** 2026-04-29
- **Decision:** filter the ESO TAP query with `REC_SETUP LIKE '%580%'` rather than `REC_SETUP = 'U580'`. The `rec_setup` column is persisted in the cache parquet so a tighter Phase 1 filter to exact equality remains available without a re-fetch.
- **Reasoning:** the LIKE pattern captures pure U580 spectra (6,071 rows on the 2026-04-29 probe) plus mixed-setup spectra including U580 (e.g. `HR10|HR21:U580`), totalling 6,143 rows with full Teff, logg, and [Fe/H]. The 1.2 percent uplift is small but free; persisting `rec_setup` lets us tighten downstream if mixed-setup rows turn out to lack a corresponding U580 spectrum in the regridded HDF5.
- **Reversal:** the Phase 1 ghost-CNAME audit (see PENDING bullet below) shows more than 5 percent of mixed-setup rows have no U580 spectrum in the HDF5; tighten the query to `REC_SETUP = 'U580'` and re-fetch.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/eso_catalog.py` (`DEFAULT_REC_SETUP_LIKE`, `build_ges_dr5_adql`, `fetch_ges_dr5_params`); `data/ges/catalogs/ges_dr5_params.parquet` cache (preserves `rec_setup` column).

If `data/pickles/` is wiped, re-run the download script; if STScI is unreachable, fall back to the ESO synphot mirror at `https://ssb.stsci.edu/cdbs/grid/pickles/dat_uvk/` which serves identical bytes.
- **Affects:** `data/pickles/*.fits` cache (gitignored per auditplan §6); Phase 5 benchmark precondition; resolves the `[PENDING Phase 5]` bullet below.

## 21. Phase 2 cross-validation strategy: 5-fold stratified, single-split as fallback

- **Date:** 2026-04-29
- **Decision:** at Phase 2, run 5-fold stratified cross-validation on the combined train+val partition (using `groups` from features.npz to keep cluster-mate spectra in the same fold per Blanco-Cuaresma 2019), report mean and std of macro-F1 across folds, then fit a final model on the full train+val and evaluate on the held-out test partition for headline metrics. Single-split (current `train_baseline` behavior in `src/interpret/classifier.py`) is the explicit fallback if implementation blocks for more than 4 hours or per-fold runtime exceeds 1 hour at the locked LightGBM hyperparameters (Decision 13).
- **Reasoning:** `project_plan.md` Phase 2 calls for 5-fold CV; auditplan §5.4 sets it as default. 5-fold provides per-class recall variance estimates that single-split cannot, which strengthens the Phase 2 acceptance gate (per-class recall greater than 0.6 on every class with >= 100 training samples). Bootstrap CIs at Phase 4 ablation provide separate uncertainty on delta-accuracy and do not substitute for fold-level variance on the baseline classifier. Group-aware splitting via the `groups` array prevents cluster-mate leakage that would inflate held-out scores (Blanco-Cuaresma 2019, MNRAS 486, 2075).
- **Reversal:** any of: (a) per-fold training time exceeds 1 hour, OR (b) total CV runtime exceeds 4 hours, OR (c) `groups` array unavailable in features.npz at Phase 2 entry. In any of these cases, fall back to single-split with the same train/val/test indices and document the reason in a new entry; bootstrap CIs at Phase 4 then carry the full uncertainty story.
- **Affects:** `src/interpret/classifier.py` (add `train_cv` function alongside the existing `train_baseline`); `scripts/train_classifier.py` (CLI flag `--cv` defaulting to 5; `--single-split` to override); `artifacts/metrics.json` (new keys `cv_macro_f1_mean`, `cv_macro_f1_std`, per-fold confusion matrices).

## 22. Phase 4 ablation gate: H Balmer / Mg b / Na D headline; Ca I as secondary

- **Date:** 2026-04-29
- **Decision:** the falsifiable Phase 4 ablation gate (auditplan §5.6) covers exactly three line sets: `H_balmer`, `Mg_b`, `Na_D`. Pass criterion: at least 2 of 3 of the headline (line_set, class) pairs have `delta_acc_ci_high < 0` AND `p_value_vs_random <= 0.01`. The `Ca_I` line set in `src/interpret/lines.py` `LINE_SETS` IS evaluated and reported in `artifacts/ablation/masked_line_ablation.csv`, but its result is presented as supporting evidence in the manuscript, not part of the falsifiable gate.
- **Reasoning:** the paper's central claim per `project_plan.md` line 5 is that masking "canonical MK diagnostic regions (H Balmer, Mg b, Na D)" produces class-specific accuracy drops. Those three are the textbook MK-typing diagnostics for FGK dwarfs (Gray and Corbally 2009): H Balmer for A and F, Mg b for F-G-K, Na D for G and K. Ca I at 6162 A and 6439 A is real physics and tracks G-K metallicity-temperature trends, but is one tier below the headline diagnostics and is not part of the original claim. Including Ca I in the gate would either weaken the gate (4-of-4 needed) or be a free pass (1-of-4 needed, easy to game). Keeping it as secondary preserves a parsimonious headline plus auditable supplementary evidence.
- **Reversal:** Phase 4 ablation reveals an unexpected `Ca_I` directional pattern (e.g. masking Ca I increases K-class accuracy, contrary to physics), in which case the inverted result is itself the headline and the gate structure is rewritten. Without that, Ca I stays secondary.
- **Affects:** `scripts/ablation.py` (CLI / report layout: headline table covers H_balmer, Mg_b, Na_D; secondary table covers Ca_I); `artifacts/ablation/masked_line_ablation.csv` schema unchanged; manuscript Section 4 framing.

## 23. Upstream src/fetch/fetch_ges.py has two bugs; corrected wrapper used instead

- **Date:** 2026-04-29
- **Decision:** the upstream sequential fetcher in `src/fetch/fetch_ges.py` (read-only per auditplan section 3) has two bugs that prevent any UVES spectra from being fetched. Phase 0 spectra acquisition uses `scripts/fetch_ges_parallel.py` instead, which inlines the fixes. Both bugs were discovered when the parallel fetch reported `hits=0` after 300 queries; manual diagnosis identified the failure mode as HTTP 400 from the ESO TAP service plus, even when the query succeeds, an `access_url` that points at a DataLink endpoint rather than the FITS file.
- **Reasoning:** Bug 1 is in `ADQL_TEMPLATE` at `src/fetch/fetch_ges.py:13-30`: the spatial columns are written as `o.ra` and `o.dec`, but the IVOA ObsCore standard at the ESO TAP service exposes them as `s_ra` and `s_dec`. Every query returns `400 Encountered "." Was expecting one of: <EOF>, ",", ";", "ASC", "DESC"` because the ADQL parser cannot resolve `o.ra` to a known column. Bug 2 is in `build_manifest` at `src/fetch/fetch_ges.py:60-80`: the code uses `best.get('access_url')` as the FITS download URL, but for ESO Phase 3 spectra the ObsCore `access_url` is a DataLink endpoint that returns a VOTable describing the dataset. The actual FITS file lives at `https://dataportal.eso.org/dataportal_new/file/<dp_id>` (verified with HTTP HEAD: 200, content-type `application/octet-stream`, content-length around 944 KB for one GES UVES spectrum). Both bugs are fixed in `scripts/fetch_ges_parallel.py` (`ADQL_TEMPLATE` constant, `query_eso_tap_fixed`, `query_one`).
- **Reversal:** if the upstream `src/fetch/fetch_ges.py` is corrected by an upstream maintainer, drop the local override and call `query_eso_tap` directly. The wrapper file should remain as a parallelized variant of `build_manifest` even after that, since the parallelism is independent of the bug fix.
- **Affects:** `scripts/fetch_ges_parallel.py` (new); `src/fetch/fetch_ges.py` (untouched but its `query_eso_tap`, `ADQL_TEMPLATE`, and `build_manifest` are not used by Phase 0 acquisition); `data/ges/manifests/ges_manifest.csv` (manifest is now built by the wrapper, not the upstream module).

## 24. UVES Phase 3 products require both chips and a custom HDF5 reader

- **Date:** 2026-04-30
- **Decision:** The original Phase 0 fetch downloaded only the U580 RED chip (582-683 nm = 5820-6830 Angstroms) per star because the ADQL used `TOP 1 ORDER BY calib_level DESC` and the database returned the red chip first. The U580 setup actually produces TWO Phase 3 products per observation, one per detector: BLUE chip (477-580 nm = 4770-5800 Angstroms) and RED chip (582-683 nm = 5820-6830 Angstroms). Combined coverage is 4770-6830 Angstroms, which contains the full 4800-6800 Angstrom window locked by Decision 1. Red chip alone covers only ~49 percent of that window and excludes Hbeta (4861 Angstroms) and the Mg b triplet (5167-5184 Angstroms), which would kill the Phase 4 headline ablation gate (Decision 22) outright. Recovery: fetch the missing blue chips with `scripts/fetch_ges_blue.py`, write `scripts/build_hdf5_phase3.py` to read the ESO Phase 3 BinTableHDU `phase3spectrum` format (a single-row table with WAVE/FLUX/ERR/QUAL columns of length 59700 in nanometers), convert nm to Angstroms, stitch blue+red per star into a single 1D spectrum, then run continuum normalization and regridding via the existing `src/preprocess/continuum.py` and `src/preprocess/wavelength_grid.py` helpers. The upstream `src/preprocess/build_hdf5.py` and its readers cannot handle this format because they expect flux as a flat float32 array in HDU0 or HDU1 image data, not as a column inside a binary table.
- **Reasoning:** Discovered when the first `src/preprocess/build_hdf5.py` run reported `Total spectra: 0, Failed spectra: 3348` with the same error per file: `Cannot cast array data from dtype((numpy.record, [('WAVE', '>f4', (59700,)), ('FLUX', '>f4', (59700,)), ('ERR', '>f4', (59700,)), ('QUAL', '>i4', (59700,))])) to dtype('float32')`. Inspecting one FITS confirmed HDU1 is a `BinTableHDU` named `phase3spectrum` with the 4-column record dtype above, and inspecting 50 FITS confirmed every file is the red chip alone (582-683 nm). A targeted ESO TAP query for the same star without `TOP 1` returned two Phase 3 products at calib_level=3, one per chip, so the missing blue chip is publicly available. Per auditplan section 3, `src/preprocess/` is read-only; the fix lives in `scripts/`. Reusing `src/preprocess/wavelength_grid.py` (`build_loglambda_grid`, `regrid_to_grid`) and `src/preprocess/continuum.py` (the percentile-method GES continuum) keeps the regridded HDF5 schema identical to what the existing `coverage_probe` and downstream Phase 1 code expect.
- **Reversal:** If chip stitching introduces seam artifacts at the inter-chip gap (5800-5820 Angstroms) that contaminate masked-line ablation results in Phase 4, mask the gap explicitly in the imputer rather than stitching. If a star's blue chip is unavailable in ESO Phase 3, drop that star from the labeled set rather than padding.
- **Affects:** new `scripts/fetch_ges_blue.py` (blue chip fetcher with `em_min < 5e-7` filter); new `scripts/build_hdf5_phase3.py` (BinTableHDU reader + nm-to-A converter + chip stitcher + regridder); existing `data/ges/uves/ges_uves_<ra>_<dec>.fits` files are repurposed as the red chip set; new `data/ges/uves/ges_uves_<ra>_<dec>_BLUE.fits` filenames distinguish the blue chip set; `data/ges/manifests/ges_manifest_blue.csv` is the new blue-chip manifest. The original red-chip manifest at `ges_manifest.csv` is retained.

## 25. scipy.ndimage.percentile_filter replaces upstream pure-Python continuum normalization

- **Date:** 2026-04-30
- **Decision:** `scripts/build_hdf5_phase3.py` defines `fast_ges_continuum_normalize` and uses it in place of `src/preprocess/continuum.apply_continuum_normalization(survey='ges')`. Both compute the same logical operation: a sliding 90th-percentile over a 150 Angstrom window, normalizing flux by the resulting continuum estimate. The 90 percent / 150 Angstrom parameters are unchanged; only the implementation differs. Method label in HDF5 metadata stays `percentile`. Quality probe (`good_continuum_fraction`) is recomputed identically, just over a vectorized output. Smoke test confirmed the normalized flux median sits at 0.93 (close to the audit's assumed 1.0) for both the old and new implementation. Per auditplan section 3, `src/preprocess/` stays read-only; the fast path lives in `scripts/`.
- **Reversal:** if a Phase 4 ablation result differs significantly between the two implementations (e.g. delta-accuracy CIs shifted by more than 0.005), re-process with the upstream method on a development branch and compare the residuals. Not expected to matter at the level of MK classification headline tests.
- **Affects:** `scripts/build_hdf5_phase3.py` (new `fast_ges_continuum_normalize` and `_worker_process_spectrum` for `concurrent.futures.ProcessPoolExecutor`); `data/common/processed/regridded_spectra.h5` schema is unchanged.

## 28. UVES inter-chip gap explicitly masked in features.npz via gap_mask key

- **Date:** 2026-04-30
- **Decision:** extend the `features.npz` schema with a boolean array `gap_mask` of shape `(n_bins,)`, True for bins whose center falls in `[5769, 5834]` A (UVES U580 inter-chip gap per Sacco et al. 2014, A&A 565, A113; bibcode 2014A&A...565A.113S) and False elsewhere. Phase 4 ablation (`scripts/ablation.py`) and Phase 3 interpretability (`scripts/run_interpret.py`) MUST consume `gap_mask` to skip those bins when reporting top-importance peaks and when drawing random-window controls. Phase 2 classifier training continues to see the full feature matrix; the mask is metadata, not a transformation.
- **Reasoning:** Phase 1 Physicist-post review (2026-04-30) identified that bins 370-388 of the rebinned grid (wave_centers 5777-5829 A) carry per-bin train medians 0.357-0.925 (mean 0.474), a 48 percent deficit relative to the rest-of-spectrum median of 0.917 (auditplan gap-imputation threshold is 10 percent). The deficit is real physics: most U580 spectra have near-zero flux in the inter-chip gap, and the train-median imputer faithfully reflects that. Any importance peak that lands in those bins during Phase 3 or Phase 4 would be an instrumental shortcut, not a physically meaningful spectral feature. Masking at interpretation time preserves Phase 2 training behaviour while keeping the audit honest. Recommendation level: chosen by Physicist-post over the alternatives of (ii) replacing imputer values with continuum=1.0 in the gap (would introduce a categorical "has flux at 5800 A" signal for the rare spectra that do carry gap flux) and (iii) keep-as-is plus caveat (more error-prone for downstream interpretation).
- **Reversal:** if a future GES data release fills the inter-chip gap and the gap-bin medians rise within 10 percent of 0.917, set `gap_mask` to all-False and re-run Phase 3 / 4 interpretation.
- **Affects:** `scripts/build_features.py` (writes `gap_mask`); `tests/test_features.py` (asserts `gap_mask` shape and `wave_centers[gap_mask]` contained in [5769, 5834] A); Phase 3 (`run_interpret.py`) and Phase 4 (`ablation.py`) consumers (must read and respect `gap_mask`); `artifacts/features.npz` schema extended from 10 to 11 keys.

## 29. Phase 2 spatial-CV sensitivity check (amendment to Decision 21)

The spatial-CV block is a sensitivity check: if `cv_spatial_macro_f1_mean` drops by more than 3 sigma below the current `cv_macro_f1_mean = 0.9126` (i.e., below 0.8772), escalate to TJ for a re-fit decision; otherwise accept and proceed.

## 30. Phase 3 shortcut-learning pivot: paper framing shifts to "audit reveals shortcut learning in tree-based stellar classifiers"

- **Date:** 2026-04-30
- **Decision:** the Phase 3 interpretability triangulation (commit 4b5a4a4 below) shows that the LightGBM classifier achieves the Phase 2 headline `macro_f1 = 0.9262` while substantially bypassing the canonical MK diagnostic system (Gray and Corbally 2009). The acceptance pathway taken is the "explicit shortcut-learning pivot" branch of auditplan §5.5, not the "Physicist-post PASS on physics-aware behaviour" branch. The Phase 3 Jaccard gate `perm_vs_shap >= 0.5` is missed by 0.0185 (observed 0.4815); both methods agree on a physics core (Hbeta cluster bins 23-26 around 4856-4866 A, Mg b triplet bins 149/153/154 at 5172.54/5182.89/5185.48 A, Halpha cluster bins 624-627 around 6559-6569 A) but disagree on off-line continuum bins. CRITICALLY, perm AND SHAP both MISS Na D (5889.95 / 5895.92 A; bins 409 and 411 rank 25-43 of 696 in K mean|SHAP|) and Ca I (6162.17 A bin 499, ranks 210/696 in K; 6439.08 A bin 587, ranks 348/696 in K) in G and K top-20 post gap-mask. The Phase 2 Gini sanity flag is corroborated. Paper framing shifts accordingly: the central claim becomes "tree-based stellar classifiers can be audited for physics-awareness via causal masked-line ablation, and this audit reveals partial shortcut learning in a UVES-trained LightGBM model" rather than "tree-based stellar classifiers ARE physics-aware".
- **Reasoning:** Physicist-post Phase 3 review (2026-04-30) returned PASS-WITH-PIVOT. The K-class SHAP top-20 leans on Halpha (6559-6569 A), Hbeta (4856-4866 A), and FeI/CrI continuum bins 123-210 (5106-5332 A; covering Mn-15 multiplet at 5269.54 A and CrI 5206/5208/5345 A per Gray and Corbally 2009 section 3.6) instead of Na D and Ca I. This is real spectral information (FeI/CrI strength tracks Teff in K-dwarfs) but it is NOT a textbook MK diagnostic. The model has learned a Teff-correlated proxy via Hbeta wing depth + FeI strength rather than the historically-defined neutral-metal diagnostics. The Hbeta/Halpha appearances in G/K top-20 are most plausibly explained by anti-correlation (very weak/filled-in Balmer cores discriminate K from F at fixed continuum) plus FeI 4859.74 / 4863.64 blends that strengthen toward K. The marginal Jaccard miss (0.4815 vs 0.5) is statistical, not physical: methods agree on the physics core; their 0.0185 gap is on near-line FeI continuation bins of secondary interest. auditplan §5.5 watchout ("If expected peaks are missing, do NOT tune until they appear. Log the negative result. The paper framing shifts to shortcut learning, which is also publishable") authorises this path explicitly.
- **Reversal:** none. If a future re-fit on a re-balanced or re-windowed sample recovers Na D and Ca I in G/K top-20 of perm AND SHAP, log a new entry that reverses this and reverts to the physics-aware framing. Otherwise the pivot is the headline finding.
- **Affects:** paper Section 1 framing (central claim); Phase 4 ablation expectations: PREDICT near-zero `delta_acc` for Na D and Ca I masking and non-zero `delta_acc` for Hbeta and Mg b masking; the Phase 4 pass criterion in auditplan section 5.6 ("at least 2 of 3 of the headline (line_set, class) pairs have delta_acc_ci_high < 0 AND p_value_vs_random <= 0.01") is reframed as a confirmatory test for Hbeta/Mg b rather than a four-way physics check; Phase 6 manuscript Section 4 (methods) re-frames the audit as a falsification probe; Phase 7 arXiv submission category astro-ph.IM remains correct. Two implementation notes for Phase 4 Coder: (1) `perm_importance.npz` is GLOBAL (1D, shape=(696,)); per-class perm projections in `triangulation_report.json` are slices of the same global ranking, NOT class-conditional. SHAP is the only genuinely per-class signal in Phase 3 artifacts. (2) Phase 4 ablation must consume Decision 28 `gap_mask` for the random-window control sampler's forbidden-mask alongside `MK_LINES`, OR the controls will land in the inter-chip gap and inflate the null distribution. (3) Phase 6 budget should include one extra paper revision pass for the framing shift (Phase 4 kill-criteria pivot in `project_plan.md` originally scheduled +2 weeks for this; resolved here at Phase 3 instead, so Phase 6 itself does not extend).

## 39. TOST indifference zone for (Mg b, K) equivalence test: +/- 0.025 delta_A

- **Date:** 2026-05-16
- **Decision:** the two one-sided t-test (TOST) equivalence band against which the (Mg b, K) masking effect is judged is set to plus/minus 0.025 in delta_acc. The band is fixed before the TOST computation runs in Step 2c of the Phase 6.1 revision. If the two one-sided 90 percent CI bounds on delta_acc_K^{(Mg_b)} both fall inside [-0.025, +0.025], we reject the null of inequivalence at alpha = 0.05 and conclude that the (Mg b, K) effect is equivalent to zero within the indifference band.
